Frequently Asked

How do I change the shortcut?
Navigate to chrome://extensions/shortcuts in Chrome. Locate **Tabs++** and enter your preferred keys.
Can I turn off Group protection?
Yes. Open the extension popup menu (click the icon) to toggle individual protections.
Will it work with vertical tab sidebars?
Yes. Tabs++ works silently in the background and respects native groups used by vertical sidebars like Sidekick or SigmaOS-style themes.
